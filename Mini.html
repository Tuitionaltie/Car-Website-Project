Classic Mini

About the car

The classic Mini is one of Britain’s most recognizable cars. Its tiny dimensions hide an incredibly clever layout: the engine is mounted sideways at the front, leaving much of the car’s length for passengers. The design became famous for being economical, fun to drive and surprisingly practical.

Quick trivia

  • The original Mini was launched in 1959 and was designed by Alec Issigonis.
  • Its transverse front-engine, front-wheel-drive layout influenced generations of small cars that followed.
  • John Cooper helped create the sportier Mini Cooper, turning the little economy car into a successful competition machine.
  • A bright green Mini became one of the most memorable running jokes in Mr Bean.
