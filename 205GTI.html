Peugeot 205 GTI

About the car

The Peugeot 205 GTI is one of the most celebrated hot hatchbacks of the 1980s. It combined a light body, responsive steering and lively engines with the practicality of a normal small hatchback. The result was a car that felt quick and playful without needing huge power.

Quick trivia

  • The 205 GTI arrived in 1984 and became one of the defining European hot hatches of its era.
  • The best-known versions used 1.6-litre and 1.9-litre petrol engines.
  • Its low weight is a big part of why the 205 GTI feels so lively compared with many newer performance cars.
  • Clean, original examples have become highly collectible.
