Nissan 240SX (S13)

About the car

The Nissan 240SX is the North American member of Nissan’s S13 family. It used a front-engine, rear-wheel-drive layout and balanced chassis that made it enjoyable even before modification. Years later, that same layout helped turn the S13 into one of the most popular platforms in drifting and tuning culture.

Quick trivia

  • The S13-generation 240SX was sold in North America from 1989 through 1994.
  • North American 240SX models used 2.4-litre KA-series engines, unlike many Japanese-market Silvia and 180SX models.
  • Its engine sits at the front while the rear wheels are driven — a layout loved by drift enthusiasts.
  • The 240SX, Silvia and 180SX are closely related S-chassis cars, but they were not identical models in every market.
