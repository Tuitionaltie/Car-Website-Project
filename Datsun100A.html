Datsun 100A

About the car

The Datsun 100A is a small Japanese hatchback from the early 1970s. Also known as the first-generation Datsun Cherry, it stood out for its compact size, simple engineering and unusual styling. It helped Datsun build a strong reputation in Europe for affordable cars that were easy to run and dependable.

Quick trivia

  • The 100A belonged to the E10-generation Datsun Cherry family, introduced in 1970.
  • It was one of Nissan/Datsun’s earliest front-wheel-drive production cars.
  • Many versions used a small 1.0-litre A-series four-cylinder engine, which suited the car’s light weight.
  • Surviving examples are now uncommon, especially in good original condition.
