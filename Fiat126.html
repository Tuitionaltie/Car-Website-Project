Fiat 126

About the car

The Fiat 126 is a tiny rear-engined city car that replaced the famous Fiat 500. It kept the idea of cheap, simple transport but wrapped it in a more modern, boxy 1970s body. In Poland it became far more than just a car: the Polski Fiat 126p became a cultural icon and earned the nickname “Maluch”, meaning “little one”.

Quick trivia

  • The Fiat 126 debuted in 1972 as the successor to the Fiat 500.
  • Its engine sits at the rear and drives the rear wheels, just like the classic Fiat 500 before it.
  • Huge numbers were built in Poland as the Polski Fiat 126p.
  • In Poland the car is widely known as the “Maluch”, which roughly means “little one”.
