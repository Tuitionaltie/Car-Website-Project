Audi 100 Coupé S

About the car

The Audi 100 Coupé S was the stylish fastback version of the early Audi 100. With its long bonnet, sweeping rear roofline and more sporting character, it was one of the cars that helped establish Audi as more than just a maker of sensible saloons. Today it is a rare and distinctive classic.

Quick trivia

  • The Coupé S arrived at the end of the 1960s and was based on the first-generation Audi 100.
  • Most examples used a 1.9-litre four-cylinder engine, giving it stronger performance than the regular 100 saloon.
  • Its fastback shape is very different from the boxier Audis that became common later in the 1970s and 1980s.
  • Because relatively few survive, the 100 Coupé S is much rarer today than many better-known German classics.
