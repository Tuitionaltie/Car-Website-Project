First-Generation Honda Civic

About the car

The first-generation Honda Civic turned Honda into a serious global car manufacturer. It was small, light, front-wheel drive and economical at exactly the right time, when fuel prices were becoming a major concern. Its simple shape and clever engineering made it a hugely important small car of the 1970s.

Quick trivia

  • The first Civic was introduced in 1972 and quickly became one of Honda’s most important models.
  • Honda’s CVCC engine technology became famous for meeting strict 1970s emissions rules without needing the same equipment as many rival cars.
  • The Civic used front-wheel drive, helping provide useful interior space despite its small exterior size.
  • The Civic name is still in production more than five decades later.
