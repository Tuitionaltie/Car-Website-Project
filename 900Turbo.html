Saab 900 Turbo

About the car

The Saab 900 Turbo mixed Swedish practicality with wonderfully strange engineering. Its long curved windscreen, front-wheel-drive layout and turbocharged engine gave it a personality unlike almost anything else on the road. It became known for strong performance, safety and unmistakably Saab design.

Quick trivia

  • The Saab 900 was introduced for the 1979 model year and developed from the earlier Saab 99.
  • Saab was one of the manufacturers that helped make turbocharging practical and popular in everyday road cars.
  • The classic 900 famously places its ignition switch between the front seats rather than on the steering column.
  • The 900’s wraparound windscreen and aircraft-inspired design details are part of its distinctive Saab character.
